# THE RECEIPT — BELL EDITION

## LINE ITEM 1 — THE CHARGE

Bell Canada applied a $6/month increase to residential internet accounts in Ontario. [CLAIM-02]

A Bell customer in Kingston received this increase on their monthly billing statement. The notice appeared on page 3 of the statement. [CLAIM-06 context — see LINE ITEM 4]

## LINE ITEM 3 — THE RULE

The CRTC Internet Code (Telecom Regulatory Policy 2019-269) requires that a provider give advance written notice before implementing a price change of this kind. [CLAIM-03]

The Internet Code specifies that this notice must be in writing. [CLAIM-03] It does not specify where within a billing statement that notice must appear. [CLAIM-03]

Bell gave written notice. The notice appeared on page 3 of the billing statement. [CLAIM-03]

The distinction in the record: notice was given. Whether notice was findable is a separate question the Internet Code does not resolve. [CLAIM-03]

---

## LINE ITEM 4 — THE RETENTION CALL

A Bell customer in Kingston contacted Bell following receipt of the increase. A Bell retention representative described a rate reduction as available and described it as permanent. [CLAIM-06]

The record does not disclose a written confirmation of the permanent rate. No written confirmation was provided by Bell at the time of the retention call. [GAP-01]

The distinction in the record: the verbal statement and any written confirmation are separate. The written confirmation is absent. [GAP-01]

---

## LINE ITEM 5 — THE REMEDY

A consumer may file a complaint with the Commission for Complaints for Telecom-television Services (CCTS). Filing is free to the consumer. The provider must respond within a fixed window. Compensation of up to a stated maximum is available. [CLAIM-04]

The record does not disclose the exact administrative cost to Bell of processing a CCTS complaint. That figure is not published at a confirmable per-complaint dollar amount. [GAP-02]

The directional claim in the record: the administrative cost to Bell of processing a CCTS complaint may exceed the $6 monthly increase. The record does not confirm this to a dollar amount. [CLAIM-05]
