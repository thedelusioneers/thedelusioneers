## LINE ITEM 1 — THE CHARGE

Bell Canada applied a $6/month increase to residential internet accounts in
Ontario. [CLAIM-02]

A Bell customer in Kingston received this increase on their monthly billing
statement. The notice of the increase appeared on page 3 of that statement.
[CLAIM-03]

Bell reported 4,455,460 retail high-speed internet subscribers in Canada at the
end of Q4 2025. [CLAIM-01] At $6 per subscriber per month, the increase
projects to approximately $26.7 million in additional monthly revenue and
approximately $320.8 million in additional annual revenue. [CLAIM-02]

## LINE ITEM 3 — THE RULE

The CRTC Internet Code (Telecom Regulatory Policy 2019-269, in force) requires
that a provider give advance written notice before implementing a price change
of this kind. [CLAIM-03]

The Internet Code specifies that notice must be in writing. [CLAIM-03] The
Internet Code does not specify where within a billing statement that notice must
appear. [CLAIM-03]

Bell gave written notice of the increase. [CLAIM-03] The notice appeared on
page 3 of the billing statement. [CLAIM-03]

The distinction the record establishes: notice was given. Whether notice was
findable is a question the Internet Code does not resolve. [CLAIM-03]

## LINE ITEM 4 — THE RETENTION CALL

A Bell customer in Kingston contacted Bell following receipt of the $6 increase.
A Bell retention representative described a rate reduction as available and
described it as permanent. [CLAIM-06]

The record does not disclose a written confirmation of the permanent rate.
No written confirmation was provided by Bell at the time of the retention call.
[GAP-01]

The distinction the record establishes: the verbal statement and any written
confirmation are separate instruments. The written confirmation is absent from
the record. [GAP-01]

---

## LINE ITEM 5 — THE REMEDY

A consumer may file a complaint with the Commission for Complaints for
Telecom-television Services (CCTS). Filing is free to the consumer. The
provider must respond within a fixed window. Compensation up to a stated
maximum is available. [CLAIM-04]

The record does not disclose the exact administrative cost to Bell of processing
a CCTS complaint. That figure is not published at a confirmable per-complaint
dollar amount. [GAP-02]

The directional finding in the record: the administrative cost to Bell of
processing a CCTS complaint may exceed the $6 monthly increase. The record does
not confirm this to a dollar amount. [CLAIM-05]
