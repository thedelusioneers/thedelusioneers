# STUB — bell-canada
# Variant: DOCUMENT

---

Bell applied a $6/month increase to Ontario internet accounts. The notice
appeared on page 3 of the billing statement. The CRTC Internet Code (Telecom
Regulatory Policy 2019-269) requires advance written notice of a price change.
It does not specify where in a bill that notice must appear. Applied across
Bell's 4,455,460 retail internet subscribers in Canada, the increase projects
to roughly $320 million in additional annual revenue. The Internet Code created
the notice requirement. Placement of that notice is outside the rule's scope.

Primary source: CRTC Telecom Regulatory Policy 2019-269 (Internet Code)
crtc.gc.ca
