# THE STUB — SPEC
The sixth deliverable. One finding, one primary source, one structural
implication stated flat, without verdict.

## Content contract
- One finding only.
- One primary source citation only.
- One structural implication, stated flat. No verdict, no evaluative language.
- Body: 100 words HARD LIMIT. Count them.
- Always links to thedelusioneers.ca.
- Platforms: Threads, Bluesky, Substack Notes, Instagram.
- Instagram variant: "link in bio" reference — no live URL.

## Variants (decided at Gate A in the assignment brief — never by a desk)
- REDACT: used when the material involves suppression or a missing record.
  Near-black ground, high contrast (Von Restorff effect — it must look unlike
  everything else in a feed).
- DOCUMENT: used otherwise. Parchment ground, consistent with the standard
  Delusioneers design system (design-tokens.md).

The variant choice is editorial judgment about the material, not aesthetic
preference. Copy-desk and art-desk execute the brief's choice; they do not
revisit it.
